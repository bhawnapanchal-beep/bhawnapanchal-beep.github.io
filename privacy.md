# Email Hub privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Email Hub is a personal tool used only by its owner, Bhawna Panchal, on her own computer. It is not available to anyone else.

## What it accesses

Only the Gmail accounts its owner chooses to connect, through Google's own permission screen.

## How the data is used

Email Hub searches and reads email so its owner can find information such as bills, receipts and attachments. Results are shown only to the owner. When the owner asks her AI assistant, Claude by Anthropic, a question about her email, the relevant email content is sent to that assistant to answer it.

## Where the data is kept

Email Hub runs on the owner's computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer. Apart from the AI assistant described above, email data is not sent to anyone else, and it is never sold or shared.

## Google API Services

Email Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account, Security, Your connections to third-party apps and services.
